# Business Sensify Completion Criteria

A Business Understanding Model is complete enough for handoff when it is decision-ready for the next phase of work.

It does not need to prove that the business will succeed.

## Required coherence

### Founder outcome

- The intended business outcome and meaningful founder constraints are explicit.
- The business model does not obviously conflict with those goals without the conflict being acknowledged.

### Customer / problem

- The target customer is specific enough to reason about.
- Buyer/user/payer roles are distinguished where material.
- The problem and current alternative/status quo are understood.
- Demand-related statements are correctly labeled as evidence-backed claims or hypotheses.

### Value / offering

- The promised outcome and offering boundaries are clear enough for downstream planning.
- The reason to switch/buy is explicit.
- Major dependencies/customer responsibilities are visible.

### Revenue / acquisition

- Who pays, for what, and how is explicit.
- Pricing decisions are separated from willingness-to-pay hypotheses.
- The intended acquisition/sales mechanism is understandable.
- Important sales incentive risks are visible.

### Operations / feasibility

- The value-delivery mechanism is understandable beyond the happy path.
- Major capacity, quality, continuity, vendor, technology, and data dependencies are visible.

### Economics

- Revenue and major cost drivers are identified.
- The model is internally coherent enough to see how incremental customers/units affect economics.
- Critical economic assumptions are explicit.
- Major cash timing requirements are visible where relevant.

### Legal / regulatory

- Material regulatory/compliance unknowns have been researched or explicitly assigned as blockers/validation needs.
- The model does not rely silently on an unverified legal assumption with critical impact.

### Risk / validation

- High-impact assumptions have been challenged.
- A premortem/red-team pass has been completed.
- Critical validation work is identified.
- The founder knows which major beliefs remain unproven.

### Decisions

- Important decisions are recorded with rationale/tradeoffs.
- Reconsideration triggers exist for decisions whose basis may change.
- Material contradictions have been resolved or consciously accepted.

## Completion summary

Before confirmation, provide a concise view of:

1. business in one coherent model;
2. critical decisions already made;
3. critical assumptions/hypotheses;
4. strongest supporting evidence;
5. biggest risks/failure modes;
6. open high-value validation work;
7. deliberately deferred questions;
8. decisions you still recommend reconsidering.

Ask the user to confirm that this accurately represents the business they intend to build.

Only then mark the Sensify phase complete and hand the model to downstream skills.
